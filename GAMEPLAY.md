# gameplay branch

Anton's personal gameplay line of this fork. It is NOT the port.

- `main` is the macOS port and follows its charter: nothing in synced code changes behaviour (AGENTS.md, docs/PORTING_PRINCIPLES.md).
- `gameplay` = `main` + gameplay changes for single-player skirmish: the `Mutators` setting (local mod archives), `SetUnitHealth{resetDecay = true}`, engine squads.
- Builder self-preservation (BuilderCAI): on a fight or patrol, builders pass over repair, resurrect and reclaim targets under known enemy fire unless they can work them from where they stand, drop an automatic job whose target comes under fire, and on patrol turn back when they come under fire heading toward the enemy. A patrol leg that leads into enemy fire is turned back before the builder is in it (it looks 300 elmos ahead), and a patrol whose current leg and next point both lie toward the enemy is dropped, also when the enemy is only ahead on the leg (a patrol that merely passes a turret dies too): the builder walks out of range, or stops where it is if it is already out of range. A repair target under fire that is within 100 elmos of build range is still worked (the builder steps over). Enemy reach is its longest weapon that can hit the builder, capped at 700, plus 100; a radar dot never seen counts as 500. Turrets are not affected. Every decision is logged in the `BuilderSafety` section.
- Threat-first targeting (CMobileCAI and AirCAI, through `GetClosestValidTarget`): a fight, patrol or idle search picks the most threatening enemy instead of the closest, where a unit with no weapons counts as 3 times as far and the unit that attacked the owner in the last 200 frames as half as far. A ground unit (CMobileCAI only, not aircraft) on an automatic attack (from a fight, patrol or idle search, never a user order) at an unarmed unit switches to an armed enemy in range, rechecked every 15 frames. Logged in the `ThreatTarget` section.
- Aircraft safety (StrafeAirMoveType, AirCAI): a plane that would land (nothing left to do, `autoLand`) stays up while an armed enemy it sees is within 1500 elmos. A patrolling or fighting plane that finds nothing to attack with an enemy within 1500 logs why (fire and move state, fighter, los, radar, valid target, weapon, distance from the patrol line). Both in the `AirSafety` section.
- `PINNED_VERSION` (`2026.07.04-0-g0000000 gameplay`) makes the engine report a version no server accepts as the public release, so a gameplay build can never join a game with official builds and desync it. It must use the dev format `<tag>-<n>-g<hex> <branch>`: UtilVersion.cmake's get_version_from_file drops anything else and the version comes out empty. Check `spring --version` after every build.
- Never PR this branch into `main` and never send it upstream. Rebase it onto `main` when the port moves.
- Builds: `make app` from this branch, installed as "BAR Launcher (dev).app" (online play stays disabled, BAR_ONLINE=0 default).
